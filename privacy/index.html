Two-Faced — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-06

App: Two-Faced (app.twofaced.symmetry), Android

Operator: 토마토하우스 (Tomato House), Korea — contact: eldora99120@gmail.com

Short version

Two-Faced works entirely on your phone. We do not collect, receive, store, or share your photos, face data, or results. The app has no account, no analytics, no ads, and no internet permission.

What the app does with your data

  • Camera. The app asks for the camera permission to show a live guide and take your selfie. Camera frames are processed on your phone and are not sent anywhere.
  • Photos you pick. If you choose a photo from your gallery, the Android system photo picker gives the app that one photo. The app does not get access to the rest of your photos or to your storage.
  • Face landmarks and results. The app finds face landmarks (points on your face) using a model bundled inside the app, then calculates the symmetry number and the mirrored images. This happens on your phone. Landmarks are numbers describing the face in that photo; they are not used to identify you or to look you up, and the app does not compare you with anyone.
  • History. Your photo, results and share card can be saved in the app's private storage on your phone so you can see them again. Only this app can read that storage. You can delete one result or all history inside the app. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.
  • Saving and sharing. If you tap "Save", the share card is saved to Pictures/Two-Faced on your phone. If you tap "Share", the card is handed to the app you pick in the Android share sheet. What happens next is governed by that app's own terms and privacy policy. Nothing is shared unless you start it.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect personal data of any kind, and we have no server that could receive it.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services, and the app does not have the INTERNET permission, so it cannot send data over the network.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties.
  • We do not use your photos or face data to train any model.
  • We do not make a face template for identification, and we do not use face data for authentication.

Third-party components

The app includes the open-source MediaPipe Face Landmarker (Apache License 2.0) and its model file, running locally. They are not connected to any online service in this app.

Children

Two-Faced is for people aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You control your local data: delete history in the app, revoke the camera permission in Android settings, or uninstall the app. If you have a question about this policy or your rights under laws such as GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, or Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (개인정보 보호법), email eldora99120@gmail.com.

Not medical or beauty advice

Two-Faced is for entertainment and self-curiosity. It is not a medical, diagnostic or cosmetic tool and does not rate attractiveness.

If this changes

If a future version adds ads or in-app purchases, the app will need an internet connection and some device or advertising data may be handled by those services (for example Google AdMob and Google Play Billing). Before that version is released, this policy and the Google Play Data safety section will be updated and the new effective date shown here. Your photos and face data will still stay on your phone.

요약

Two-Faced는 모든 처리를 내 폰 안에서 합니다. 사진, 얼굴 데이터, 결과를 수집·전송·저장·공유하지 않아요. 계정, 분석 도구, 광고가 없고 앱에 인터넷 권한도 없습니다.

앱이 데이터를 다루는 방식

  • 카메라. 실시간 안내와 셀카 촬영을 위해 카메라 권한을 요청합니다. 카메라 화면은 폰 안에서만 처리되고 어디로도 보내지 않아요.
  • 직접 고른 사진. 갤러리에서 사진을 고르면 안드로이드 시스템 사진 선택기가 그 사진 한 장만 앱에 전달합니다. 다른 사진이나 저장공간에는 접근하지 않아요.
  • 얼굴 랜드마크와 결과. 앱에 들어 있는 모델로 얼굴 랜드마크(얼굴 위의 점)를 찾고 대칭 지수와 거울 합성 이미지를 계산합니다. 이 과정은 폰 안에서 이뤄져요. 랜드마크는 그 사진 속 얼굴을 나타내는 숫자일 뿐이며, 사용자를 식별하거나 다른 사람과 비교하는 데 쓰지 않습니다.
  • 기록. 사진, 결과, 공유 카드는 다시 볼 수 있도록 앱 전용 저장공간(폰 내부)에 저장될 수 있어요. 이 저장공간은 이 앱만 읽을 수 있습니다. 앱 안에서 결과 하나 또는 전체 기록을 지울 수 있고, 앱을 삭제하면 저장된 것이 모두 사라집니다.
  • 저장과 공유. "저장"을 누르면 공유 카드가 폰의 Pictures/Two-Faced 폴더에 저장돼요. "공유"를 누르면 안드로이드 공유 화면에서 고른 앱으로 카드가 전달됩니다. 그 뒤의 처리는 선택한 앱의 약관과 개인정보처리방침을 따라요. 사용자가 직접 시작하지 않는 한 아무것도 공유되지 않습니다.
